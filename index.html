    Questions

    Before you book.

    Will customers know they're talking to an agency?

    No. We reply under your brand, in your tone, from your helpdesk. To your customers, we are your support team.

    Does AI reply to my customers on its own?

    AI drafts and researches; our team reviews and sends. You decide which kinds of replies, if any, can go out automatically.

    What access do you need?

    Staff or collaborator access to your store, your helpdesk and your payment dispute centers. You choose the permission level, and you can remove it anytime.

    How fast can we start?

    Most brands are live within a week of the strategy call. Urgent dispute backlogs can usually start sooner.

    Do you lock me into a long contract?

    No. Plans run month to month, so we keep earning your business.
