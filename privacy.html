Privacy policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

What we collect

When you send the contact form on assistaa.com, we collect your name, work email, store URL, the service you're interested in and any note you add.

How we use it

We use it only to reply to you, prepare your Store X-Ray and discuss working together. We don't sell or rent your information, and we don't add you to marketing lists without your permission.

Assistaa Shopify app

If you install the Assistaa app on your Shopify store, you choose what Assistaa can access. Products and orders (view) are always on; stock and product cost, customers, payment disputes, store analytics, order edits and discounts are optional, and you can switch each one on or off at any time inside the app. Billing is never included. Every change Assistaa makes to your store is shown to you first with the current value next to the new one, and nothing is applied until you approve it, unless you have set that type of change to automatic. Every change is logged and you can reset any of them. We do not save your customers' details: they are read live from Shopify when needed. Your store's access key is kept encrypted. If you uninstall the app our access stops at once, and when Shopify asks us to delete your store's data (48 hours later) we do so automatically.

Data processing terms for merchants

These terms apply to every store that installs the Assistaa app or gives Assistaa access in any other way. The store owner is the controller of their store's data, including their customers' personal data; Assistaa processes it only on the owner's behalf and only to deliver the services agreed with them. We use the minimum data needed, only for the purpose it was granted for, and never sell it, use it for advertising or share it with third parties other than the services that host our systems (Supabase and Vercel). Access inside Assistaa is limited to the owner and admins, and to team members who need it for the agreed work. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest. We keep the change log for as long as you use Assistaa and delete your store's data when you uninstall the app or end the engagement. We pass on customer privacy requests that Shopify sends us (data requests and deletion requests) and handle them without delay. If we ever learn of a security incident affecting your store's data, we tell you promptly. You can ask questions about these terms or request a copy of your store's data at edu.atique@gmail.com.

Store X-Ray

When you request a Store X-Ray we collect your store address, email and WhatsApp number, plus a one-way hash of your connection address to stop abuse. We email you a one-time code to confirm the request, then read only the public pages and public product data of your store. Your report is stored so you can open it again from the link we send you, and we may follow up about it by email or WhatsApp. One free X-Ray is offered per store and per person.

Where it's stored

Form submissions are stored securely with our database provider (Supabase) and are only accessible to the Assistaa team.

Client data

When we work for a store, we access its systems only with the permissions the store owner grants, follow their policies, and remove our access when the engagement ends.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct or delete the information you sent us at any time by replying to any email from us.
