Privacy policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

What we collect

When you send the contact form on assistaa.com, we collect your name, work email, store URL, the service you're interested in and any note you add.

How we use it

We use it only to reply to you, prepare your Store X-Ray and discuss working together. We don't sell or rent your information, and we don't add you to marketing lists without your permission.

Where it's stored

Form submissions are stored securely with our database provider (Supabase) and are only accessible to the Assistaa team.

Client data

When we work for a store, we access its systems only with the permissions the store owner grants, follow their policies, and remove our access when the engagement ends.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct or delete the information you sent us at any time by replying to any email from us.
